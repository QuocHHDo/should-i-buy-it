Should I Buy It?

Shows a verdict on Walmart, Sam's Club, Costco, Target and Kroger product pages, using these goals. Everything stays in this browser.

Bring your goals from the app

In the app, open Goals → Your data and choose Copy as text or Copy transfer link, then paste it here. Only your goals, skip list, mild-food setting, allergies and diet are used.

Your goals

Food allergies (flagged on every product; always read the package)
Diet (flagged the same way)

Search results

Search pages don't include nutrition, so the extension opens each product's page in the background: only products you scroll to, one about every 1.5 seconds, at most 40 per page, and remembered for a week. Leave this off if pages feel slow.

When there's no rating row

The verdict normally sits next to a product's star rating. On the rare page without one, it shows as a small floating pill instead. Where should that pill go?

Either way, it moves out of the way rather than cover Add to cart, Buy now or checkout.
